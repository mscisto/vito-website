01 / Sustainability / Dissertation

Sustainable B2B packaging

In progress

I am currently working on a dissertation in collaboration with Spirax Sarco to design packaging for B2B transportation between their sister companies in the EU that comply with the recent Packing and Packaging Waste Regulations (PPWR) that have been put in place. I aim to design a short, medium, and long-term plan to implement packaging solutions that align with the PPWR timeline as well as create a prototype to demonstrate the performance of the packaging.
